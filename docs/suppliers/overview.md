# Suppliers

The **Suppliers** module keeps track of all the companies you buy or hire equipment from. Manage contact details, account info, and see related orders, assets, and sub-hires in one place.

## Supplier list

Go to **Suppliers** in the sidebar to see every supplier. The list shows:

- Supplier name
- Contact name
- Email and phone
- Account number
- Status (active / inactive)

Click a supplier to open their detail page.

## Adding a supplier

1. Go to **Suppliers** and click **Add Supplier**
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required)
   - Contact name, email, phone
   - Website, address
   - Account number and payment terms
   - Default lead time
   - Notes and tags
3. Save

## Editing a supplier

Open a supplier's detail page and click **Edit**. Update any field and save.

## Supplier detail page

The detail page has three tabs:

| Tab | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | Purchase orders placed with this supplier |
| **Assets** | Equipment owned by or linked to this supplier |
| **Sub-hires** | Sub-hire orders placed with this supplier |

Info cards at the top show contact details, account information, and a summary.

## Permissions

| Role | Supplier access |
|---|---|
| Owner / Admin | Full access |
| Manager | Create, read, update |
| Member / Staff / Viewer | Read only |

## Deleting a supplier

You can't delete a supplier that has linked assets, line items, or orders. Remove those links first, then delete.
