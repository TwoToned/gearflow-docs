# Delivery Dockets

A delivery docket is the document your crew takes to site. It lists every piece of equipment being delivered, with checkboxes to mark items as received and later as returned.

## What's on a delivery docket

- **Project details** — project name, number, venue, site contact
- **Equipment list** — every item grouped by category, with quantities, model names, and serial numbers
- **Checkboxes** — a tick box per item for on-site delivery check-off
- **Asset tags** — serial numbers / asset tags shown per unit

### Two-column signature layout

The bottom of the docket has a signature section split into two columns:

| Left column | Right column |
|---|---|
| Delivered by (crew signature) | Received by (client signature) |
| Date / time delivered | Date / time received |
| Delivery notes | Return notes |

This lets the same physical docket serve delivery and return on a single sheet.

## Generating a delivery docket

1. Open a project
2. Click **Documents** in the top bar
3. Select **Delivery Docket**

GearFlow filters the equipment to show only checked-out items. If nothing has been deployed yet, the docket will be empty — deploy gear from the warehouse first.

## Deployment-aware filtering

Only equipment with a **Checked Out** status appears on the delivery docket. Items still in prep or not yet picked are excluded.
