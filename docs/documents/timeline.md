# Timeline PDF

The timeline PDF is the printable logistics schedule for a project — a date-grouped run of every service from first delivery to final pickup. Where the [run sheet](./run-sheets.md) is the live screen your crew checks on site, the timeline is the branded document you file, print for the production office, or send to a client who wants the day-by-day plan in writing.

It pulls the project's services straight from the project, so it always matches the current schedule — no separate planning sheet to keep in sync.

## What's on the timeline

A portrait A4 document listing the project's services grouped by date. Each service row shows:

| Column | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Delivery, pickup, bump-in, bump-out, labour, or misc |
| **Title** | The service description |
| **Time** | When it's scheduled |
| **Crew** | Crew assigned to the service |
| **Cost** | The service's cost |

Your organisation's logo and footer are applied automatically through the standard document branding.

## Generating a timeline

1. Open the project.
2. Click **Documents** > **Timeline**.

GearFlow builds the PDF from the project's services and opens it in a new tab to review.

Take a corporate conference with a tight venue window: a Thursday-evening delivery and rigging bump-in, a Friday show day with running labour, and a Saturday strike and pickup. The timeline groups each day's services together — type, time, crew, and cost down the page — so the venue's events manager has the whole movement plan on one sheet and knows exactly when your trucks are on their dock.

:::note
The timeline lists **services** — the logistics tasks on a project (deliveries, bump-in/out, labour, pickups). To add or adjust these, use the Labour & Logistics tab on the project. If a project has no services, the timeline will be empty.
:::
