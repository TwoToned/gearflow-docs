# Getting Started

This guide walks you through creating your GearFlow account, setting up your organisation, and getting oriented with the dashboard.

![GearFlow Dashboard — your command centre for equipment, projects, and warehouse operations](/img/screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Creating your account

1. Go to [gearflow.app](https://gearflow.app) and click **Get Started** or navigate directly to the registration page.

2. Enter your **full name**, **email address**, and create a **password**. Your password must be at least 8 characters.

3. Click **Create Account**.

4. GearFlow sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open the email and click **Verify Email**.

If you don't see the email, check your spam folder. You can request a new verification email from the login page.

## Creating your organisation

Once your email is verified, you'll be prompted to set up your organisation:

1. Enter your **organisation name** — this is usually your company name (e.g. "Acme AV Rentals").

2. Choose your **organisation slug** — this is used in your GearFlow URL (e.g. `acme-av`). You can change this later in Settings.

3. Click **Create Organisation**.

You're now logged into GearFlow. You'll land on the **Dashboard**.

## First login

After setting up your organisation, the dashboard greets you with an overview of your rental operations. If you've been invited to an existing organisation, you'll land on the dashboard immediately after accepting the invite.

## Dashboard tour

The dashboard is your home base. It's split into several sections:

### Active Projects

Shows all projects currently in progress. Each card displays the project name, client, date range, and a quick status indicator. Click any project to open it.

### Warehouse Status

A snapshot of what's currently deployed and what's available. You'll see counts for:
- **Assets out on deployment** — gear that's checked out to projects
- **Assets available** — gear in the warehouse ready to go
- **Items in prep** — gear being prepared for upcoming projects

### Upcoming Deployments

Lists projects with deployments scheduled in the next few days. Each entry shows the project name, client, deployment date, and the number of items to deploy. Click **View Pull Sheet** to see the full list.

### Recent Activity

A running log of recent actions taken in your organisation — items deployed, returned, projects created, and inventory changes. This updates in real time as your team works.
