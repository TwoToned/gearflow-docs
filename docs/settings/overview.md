# Settings

The **Settings** page is where you configure your organisation — from team members and branding to integrations and billing.

Settings are the control panel for how your whole rental operation runs. This is where you decide who can do what — team permissions for your warehouse crew versus your office staff — and how GearFlow represents your business to the outside world, like the branding on quotes going out to venue clients. Getting these right up front means everything downstream (asset numbering, document templates, notifications) behaves the way your business expects.

![The Settings area, where you configure your organisation](/img/screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Settings sections

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **General** | Organisation name, slug, and timezone |
| **Inventory** | Asset tag prefix and numbering format, default categories |
| **Projects** | Default project type, auto-numbering, quoting preferences |
| **Team** | Invite members, manage roles, remove people |
| **Branding** | Logo, colours, document templates, platform name |
| **Billing** | Subscription plan, payment method, invoices |
| **Check Items** | Create and manage the quality check item library |
| **Test & Tag** | Test profiles, default intervals, label settings |
| **Documents** | Document template management and customisation |
| **Integrations** | WooCommerce, Discord, SSO, and social login configuration |
| **SSO** | Single Sign-On with SAML 2.0 and OIDC |
| **Notifications** | Configure which events trigger notifications and who receives them |

## Who can access settings

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full access to all settings |
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings |
| **Manager** | Most settings, except org-level delete operations |
| **Member** | Read-only on some settings sections |
| **Viewer** | No access to settings |

## Navigating settings

Settings are organised into cards grouped by topic. Use the sidebar to jump between sections, or use the command palette (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K) to find a specific setting page quickly.

## Organisation data

Your organisation's data is private to your team. Settings that affect data export or transfer (like CSV import/export) are available from the relevant feature pages as well as from Settings.
