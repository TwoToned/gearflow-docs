Settings

The Settings page is where you configure your organisation — from team members and branding to integrations and billing.

Settings are the control panel for how your whole rental operation runs. This is where you decide who can do what — team permissions for your warehouse crew versus your office staff — and how GearFlow represents your business to the outside world, like the branding on quotes going out to venue clients. Getting these right up front means everything downstream (asset numbering, document templates, notifications) behaves the way your business expects.

Settings sections​

SectionWhat you can do
GeneralOrganisation name, slug, and timezone
InventoryAsset tag prefix and numbering format, default categories
ProjectsDefault project type, auto-numbering, quoting preferences
TeamInvite members, manage roles, remove people
BrandingLogo, colours, document templates, platform name
BillingSubscription plan, payment method, invoices
Check ItemsCreate and manage the quality check item library
Test & TagTest profiles, default intervals, label settings
DocumentsDocument template management and customisation
IntegrationsWooCommerce, Discord, SSO, and social login configuration
SSOSingle Sign-On with SAML 2.0 and OIDC
NotificationsConfigure which events trigger notifications and who receives them

Who can access settings​

RoleAccess
OwnerFull access to all settings
AdminFull access to all settings
ManagerMost settings, except org-level delete operations
MemberRead-only on some settings sections
ViewerNo access to settings

Navigating settings​

Settings are organised into cards grouped by topic. Use the sidebar to jump between sections, or use the command palette (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K) to find a specific setting page quickly.

Organisation data​

Your organisation's data is private to your team. Settings that affect data export or transfer (like CSV import/export) are available from the relevant feature pages as well as from Settings.
