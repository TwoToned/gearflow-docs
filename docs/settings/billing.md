# Billing

The **Billing** section in Settings lets you manage your GearFlow subscription.

## Subscription plan

Your current plan and its features are displayed at the top of the billing page. Here you can:

- **View your plan** — see which plan you're on and what's included
- **Upgrade or downgrade** — switch between plans as your needs change
- **See usage** — view your current usage against plan limits

## Payment method

Manage your payment details:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. In the **Payment Method** section, view the current card on file.
3. Click **Update** to change the card details.
4. Enter the new card information (number, expiry, CVC).

Payment details are processed securely through the payment gateway. GearFlow does not store full card numbers.

## Invoices

View and download past invoices:

1. In **Settings > Billing**, scroll to the **Invoices** section.
2. Invoices are listed by date with amounts and status.
3. Click any invoice to view or download a PDF copy.

Invoices are generated at each billing cycle. If you need a copy of an invoice for a specific period, you can find it here.

## Changing your plan

1. Click **Change Plan** on the billing page.
2. Review the available plans and their features.
3. Select the plan you want to switch to.
4. Confirm the change.

If upgrading, the new plan takes effect immediately and you're charged the prorated difference. If downgrading, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancelling

To cancel your subscription:

1. Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the billing page.
2. Follow the prompts to confirm.

After cancellation, you'll have access until the end of your current billing period. After that, your organisation will be downgraded to a free tier or suspended depending on the plan terms.

## Need help?

If you have billing questions or need to discuss enterprise pricing, contact the GearFlow team through the support channel available in the app.
