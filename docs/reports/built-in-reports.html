Built-in Reports

Built-in reports are GearFlow's answers to the questions every rental manager asks on a Monday morning — what's overdue, who owes us money, which gear is sitting idle, where the maintenance budget went. There are roughly 30 of them, grouped by category in the report library, and each one runs against your live data with a single click. No setup, no query building.

Reach for these before reaching for the custom builder. If a venue calls asking which of their last three shows still has gear out, Overdue Returns answers it on the spot. Planning next quarter's purchases? Model Popularity tells you which fixtures get booked over and over so you stop sub-hiring them. Every report exports to CSV for the accountant or PDF for a client, and any of them can be saved with your own filters for next time.

Inventory reports​

ReportWhat it shows
Asset InventoryFull list of all assets with status, category, model, and serial number
Asset UtilisationBooking rates and revenue per asset over a selected period (see Utilization)
Model PopularityWhich equipment models are rented most frequently, by project count
Asset Value by CategoryTotal purchase value of assets grouped by category
Warranty ExpiryAssets approaching or past their warranty expiry date

Project reports​

ReportWhat it shows
Project SummaryOverview of all projects with status, client, dates, and total value
Revenue by ClientTotal revenue generated per client over a date range
Overdue ReturnsProjects or line items that are past their return date
Projects by LocationProjects grouped by venue or location
Subhire SummaryAll sub-hired equipment across projects with costs
Project ProfitabilityRevenue minus costs per project

Kit reports​

Client reports​

Maintenance reports​

ReportWhat it shows

Crew reports​

ReportWhat it shows
Crew RosterAll crew members with their roles, certifications, and contact details
Crew UtilisationHours worked and project assignments per crew member
Labour Cost by ProjectTotal labour cost per project from crew assignments
Freelancer SpendTotal spend on freelance crew members in a date range

Operations reports​

ReportWhat it shows
Activity LogA chronological log of all actions in the organisation
Location SummaryEquipment and project counts grouped by location

Running a report​

  1. Go to Reports.
  2. Browse the Pre-Built section or search by name.
  3. Click any report to open it.
  4. Set any date range or filter options.
  5. Click Run to see results.
  6. Export via the CSV or PDF buttons.

Each report runs against your current data. Results are paginated for fast loading. You can save a pre-built report as a new saved report with customised filters for quick access later.
