# Built-in Reports

GearFlow ships with approximately 30 pre-built reports covering every area of your rental operation. They're grouped by category in the report library.

## Inventory reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Asset Inventory** | Full list of all assets with status, category, model, and serial number |
| **Asset Utilisation** | Booking rates and revenue per asset over a selected period (see **[Utilization](./utilization.md)**) |
| **Model Popularity** | Which equipment models are rented most frequently, by project count |
| **Asset Value by Category** | Total purchase value of assets grouped by category |
| **Warranty Expiry** | Assets approaching or past their warranty expiry date |

## Project reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Project Summary** | Overview of all projects with status, client, dates, and total value |
| **Revenue by Client** | Total revenue generated per client over a date range |
| **Overdue Returns** | Projects or line items that are past their return date |
| **Projects by Location** | Projects grouped by venue or location |
| **Subhire Summary** | All sub-hired equipment across projects with costs |
| **Project Profitability** | Revenue minus costs per project |

## Kit reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Kit Inventory** | All kits with their contents, quantities, and status |
| **Kit Utilisation** | Booking rates and revenue per kit |

## Client reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Client Revenue** | Revenue per client with totals |
| **Top Clients** | Highest-revenue clients ranked |

## Maintenance reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Maintenance Costs by Type** | Total maintenance spend grouped by maintenance type (Repair, Preventative, etc.) |
| **Open Maintenance** | All maintenance records that are currently in progress or scheduled |

## Crew reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Crew Roster** | All crew members with their roles, certifications, and contact details |
| **Crew Utilisation** | Hours worked and project assignments per crew member |
| **Labour Cost by Project** | Total labour cost per project from crew assignments |
| **Freelancer Spend** | Total spend on freelance crew members in a date range |

## Operations reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Activity Log** | A chronological log of all actions in the organisation |
| **Location Summary** | Equipment and project counts grouped by location |

## Running a report

1. Go to **Reports**.
2. Browse the **Pre-Built** section or search by name.
3. Click any report to open it.
4. Set any date range or filter options.
5. Click **Run** to see results.
6. Export via the **CSV** or **PDF** buttons.

Each report runs against your current data. Results are paginated for fast loading. You can save a pre-built report as a new saved report with customised filters for quick access later.
