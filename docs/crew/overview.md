# Crew

The **Crew** module lets you manage all the people who work on your projects — employees, freelancers, contractors, and volunteers. Track their contact details, roles, skills, certifications, availability, and assignments.

## Crew list

Go to **Crew** in the sidebar to see every crew member in your organisation. The list shows:

- Name and profile picture (or initials if none uploaded)
- Role
- Department
- Status (Active, Inactive, On Leave, Archived)
- Contact info

Click a crew member to open their detail page.

## Adding a crew member

1. Go to **Crew** and click **Add Crew Member**
2. Fill in the details:
   - First and last name
   - Email and phone
   - Type: Employee, Freelancer, Contractor, or Volunteer
   - Role and department
   - Default day rate, hourly rate, and currency
   - Emergency contact
   - Notes and tags
3. Save

You can also link a crew member to a platform user account. Linked crew members inherit the user's name, email, and profile picture.

## Roles and skills

**Roles** (e.g. "Audio Tech", "Rigger", "Project Manager") define what a crew member does. Manage roles in **Crew** > **Roles & Skills**.

**Skills** are tags for capabilities (e.g. "Midas M32", "Forklift", "Scaffold"). Assign multiple skills to each crew member.

## Profile pictures

Upload a profile picture from the crew member's detail page (hover over the avatar). If no picture is set, the first and last initials are shown. Crew members linked to a user account inherit the user's profile picture.

## Permissions

| Role | Crew access |
|---|---|
| Owner / Admin | Full access — create, read, update, delete |
| Manager | Create, read, update |
| Member / Staff / Warehouse / Viewer | Read only |
