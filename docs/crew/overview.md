# Crew

The **Crew** module is your people database — every employee, freelancer, contractor, and volunteer you staff onto jobs, with their roles, skills, certifications, rates, availability, and project assignments in one place. When a festival weekend needs an FOH engineer, two riggers, and a system tech, this is where you find out who's qualified, who's free, and what they cost before you send the offer.

Gear is only half a rental. The other half is the crew who load it, fly it, patch it, and run it — and that side moves just as fast. A lighting op cancels, a rigger's working-at-height ticket lapses the week of a show, two jobs both want your best monitor engineer on the same Saturday. Tracking crew in GearFlow means you know at a glance who can do the work, who's already booked, and what the labour is going to cost the project, so staffing never becomes the thing that derails a job.

## Crew list

Go to **Crew** in the sidebar to see every crew member in your organisation. The list shows:

- Name and profile picture (or initials if none uploaded)
- Role
- Department
- Status (Active, Inactive, On Leave, Archived)
- Contact info

Click a crew member to open their detail page, where you'll find their rates, skills, assignments, availability, certifications, time entries, and calendar feed.

## Adding a crew member

1. Go to **Crew** and click **Add Crew Member**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - First and last name
   - Email and phone
   - **Type** — Employee, Freelancer, Contractor, or Volunteer
   - **Role** and department (e.g. role "Monitor Engineer", department "Audio")
   - **Default day rate**, hourly rate, overtime multiplier, and currency
   - Emergency contact
   - Notes and tags
3. Save.

For example, a freelance rigger might be set up as type **Freelancer**, role **Rigger**, department **Rigging**, with a default day rate and a "Working at Heights" skill tag — so when a job needs flying points, you can find them in seconds.

### Linking to a platform user

The **Platform Account** card is the first thing on the crew form. Link a crew member to a platform user and GearFlow auto-fills their first name, last name, and email, and the crew profile inherits the user's name, email, and profile picture. This is how a staff member who logs into GearFlow and the crew record you roster onto jobs become the same person.

Linked crew members can always view **their own** crew profile — even without crew read permission — via the **My Crew Profile** link in the user menu.

## Roles and skills

**Roles** define what a crew member does — FOH Engineer, Monitor Engineer, Rigger, Lighting Op, System Tech, Stagehand, Project Manager. A role can carry a default rate and rate type, so a newly added rigger inherits the right day rate automatically. Manage roles in **Crew** > **Roles & Skills**.

**Skills** are capability tags — "Midas M32", "Forklift", "Scaffold", "Working at Heights". Assign as many as apply to each person. When you build a crew for a job, the assignment picker shows each person's role and skills so you can put the right hands on the right gear.

## Profile pictures

Upload a profile picture from the crew member's detail page by hovering over the avatar. If no picture is set, the first and last initials are shown. Crew members linked to a user account inherit the user's profile picture instead.

## How crew fit a project

You don't assign crew from the Crew module — you do it from the job. On a project's **Crew** tab you add assignments (crew member, role, phase, dates, and an optional rate override), send offers, and confirm bookings. The project's financial summary then shows the **labour cost** alongside the equipment rental. The Crew module is where you maintain the people; the project is where you put them to work.

## Permissions

| Role | Crew access |
|---|---|
| Owner / Admin | Full access — create, read, update, delete |
| Manager | Create, read, update |
| Member / Staff / Warehouse / Viewer | Read only |

Any user with a linked crew profile can always view their own profile regardless of these permissions.
