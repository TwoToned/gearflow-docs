# Certifications & Skills

**Certifications** track the qualifications, tickets, and licences your crew hold — and, crucially, when they expire. **Skills** tag crew members with specific capabilities. Together they make sure the right person ends up on the right job, and that nobody gets rostered onto work they're no longer qualified to do.

In live events the wrong answer here is expensive — or dangerous. You can't fly a truss with a rigger whose working-at-height ticket lapsed last month, and you don't want to find that out the morning of the bump-in. GearFlow watches expiry dates for you and warns you before a ticket runs out, so a lapsed certification is something you renew in advance, not something you discover on site.

## Adding a certification

On a crew member's detail page, open the **Certifications** tab and click **Add Certification**.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Name | "Working at Heights", "EWP Licence", "First Aid", "White Card" |
| Issued by | The training body or authority |
| Certificate number | The reference on the ticket |
| Issued date | When it was granted |
| Expiry date | When it lapses — drives expiry tracking |

For example, a rigger's profile might carry a Working at Heights ticket and an EWP licence, each with its own expiry date, so you can see at a glance that they're cleared for the flying work on a festival main stage.

## Certification statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | Not expired and verified |
| **Expiring Soon** | Within 30 days of the expiry date |
| **Expired** | Past the expiry date |
| **Not Verified** | No verification check done |

## Expiry tracking

GearFlow tracks expiry dates automatically. When a certification falls **within 30 days** of expiring, its status flips to **Expiring Soon** and GearFlow sends a reminder. Expired certifications are flagged in red on the crew member's profile and in the crew list, so a lapsed ticket is impossible to miss.

This is the safety net that matters most. Say you're building a crew for a festival weekend six weeks out and your lead rigger's height ticket expires in three: the Expiring Soon flag and the reminder give you a clear runway to get them renewed before they're standing under a grid.

## Skills

Skills are freeform capability tags — "Midas M32", "DiGiCo", "Forklift", "Scaffold", "Audio". Assign as many as apply to each crew member to describe what they can actually do, beyond their job title.

Manage skills in **Crew** > **Roles & Skills** — create them there, then assign them to crew from each member's detail page. A monitor engineer might carry "DiGiCo SD-series" and "RF coordination" tags; a stagehand might carry "Forklift" and "Working at Heights".

## Using certifications and skills when staffing

When you add crew to a project, the assignment picker shows each person's role and skills alongside their name. Skills aren't yet used for automated matching, but having them on the profile means you can make an informed call on the spot — picking the system tech who knows your console rather than the one who doesn't.

Pair that with availability and certification status and the staffing decision comes together: the right skills, free on the dates, and a current ticket for the work.

## Notifications

You'll receive in-app notifications when a crew member's certification is within 30 days of expiry. Managers also see the count of expiring certifications in the crew dashboard stats — a standing reminder to chase renewals before they become a problem on site.
