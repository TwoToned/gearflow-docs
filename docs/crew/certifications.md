# Certifications & Skills

Certifications let you track qualifications, tickets, and licences for your crew. Skills tag crew members with specific capabilities. Together they help you assign the right person to the right job.

## Adding a certification

On a crew member's detail page, go to the **Certifications** tab and click **Add Certification**.

Fields:

- Name (e.g. "EWP Licence", "Working at Heights", "First Aid")
- Issued by
- Certificate number
- Issued date
- Expiry date

Certifications can have one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Current | Not expired and verified |
| Expiring Soon | Within 30 days of expiry |
| Expired | Past the expiry date |
| Not Verified | No verification check done |

## Expiry tracking

GearFlow tracks certification expiry dates automatically. When a certification is within 30 days of expiring, its status changes to **Expiring Soon** and GearFlow sends a reminder notification.

Expired certifications are marked in red on the crew member's profile and in the crew list.

## Skills

Skills are freeform tags like "Midas M32", "Forklift", "Scaffold", "Audio". Assign them to crew members to describe their capabilities.

Manage skills in **Crew** > **Roles & Skills**. Create skills and assign them to crew members from their detail page.

## Use in crew selection

When adding crew to a project, the assignment picker shows each person's role and skills. Skills aren't yet used for automated matching, but having them on the profile helps you make informed decisions.

## Notifications

You'll receive in-app notifications when a crew member's certification is expiring within 30 days. Managers see expiring certifications in the crew dashboard stats.
