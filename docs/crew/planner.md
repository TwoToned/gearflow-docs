# Crew Planner

The **Crew Planner** is a 14-day Gantt-style timeline of every active crew member's assignments and availability, all on one screen. It answers the question you ask a dozen times a week — "who's free?" — without phone calls or a whiteboard.

Staffing a busy season is a balancing act. You might be rostering an FOH engineer and two riggers for a festival weekend while a separate crew runs a corporate gala across town and your lighting op is mid-way through a theatre season. The planner lays all of that out as a grid: people down the side, dates across the top, coloured blocks where they're committed. One glance tells you that your best monitor engineer is already on the festival that Saturday, so the gala needs someone else — before you make a promise you can't keep.

## Opening the planner

Go to **Crew** > **Planner** (or click the **Planner** link under Crew in the sidebar).

The timeline shows:

- **Crew names** — a sticky left column that stays put as you scroll
- **Date columns** — 14 days at a time, scrollable
- **Assignment cells** — coloured blocks showing when someone is committed to a project
- **Availability blocks** — colour-coded by type

## Colour coding

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Primary (blue) | Active assignment |
| Red | Unavailable — hard block |
| Amber | Tentative — soft block, or double-booked |
| Green | Preferred — wants work |

Hover over any cell for a tooltip with the project name, role, and times — or the reason on an availability block. So when you see amber on your lighting op's row mid-season, the tooltip tells you it's a tentative hold for a wedding, not a hard "no".

## Navigating

- **Back / Forward** arrows move the window by a week.
- **Today** snaps back to the current date.
- Weekend columns and today's column are highlighted so the eye finds them instantly.

## Spotting conflicts before they bite

The planner is the fastest way to catch a clash. A red block means the person has flagged themselves unavailable; amber means a tentative hold or that they're already assigned elsewhere on that day. If you try to assign someone over one of those blocks, GearFlow surfaces a conflict warning in the assignment dialog — hard conflicts (Unavailable) in red, soft conflicts (Tentative or double-booked) in amber. You can still override and assign — handy when a freelancer confirms they can shuffle a tentative hold — but you do it knowingly.

## Where assignments come from

The planner is **read-only** — it shows the picture, it doesn't change it. To create or edit a booking, open the project's **Crew** tab and add the assignment there (crew member, role, phase, dates). It then appears on the planner the next time you view it.

## Availability blocks

The coloured availability you see in the planner is set on each crew member's detail page, in the **Availability** tab:

- **Unavailable** — a hard block (on leave, on another job, simply not free), shown in red
- **Tentative** — a soft hold, shown in amber
- **Preferred** — actively wants work in this window, shown in green

Keeping these current is what makes the planner trustworthy. A freelance rigger who marks themselves Preferred for a festival weekend is telling you to book them first.
